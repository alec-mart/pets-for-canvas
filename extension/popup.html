Settings & data
What leaves your browser, and why

Your coins, streak rewards, collars and companions are kept on our server under an anonymous device id (a random string, not tied to your name or Canvas account) so they can't be tampered with. Earning coins sends the number of the assignment you submitted, with the date, or the streak milestone you hit; shopping sends what you picked, including the name you give your pet.

To keep your pet across devices, the extension saves that device id in your Canvas account's private app storage. Only you can read it. It contains nothing about you.

If you send feedback from Settings, we store the message with your device id and the extension version.

Never sent: your name, email, Canvas account, grades, submissions, messages, or assignment titles. The pet reads your course list and overdue status from Canvas to know how you're doing; that stays in this browser.

To delete your answers, email alecmartinnnn@gmail.com with your device id. Full privacy policy.
